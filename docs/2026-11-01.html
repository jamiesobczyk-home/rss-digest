Leaking Secrets from Air-Gap Computers by Spelling Radio Signals from RAM (2024)

Researchers have demonstrated RAMBO, an attack that leaks sensitive information from air-gapped (isolated) computers by generating covert radio signals from the computer's RAM. Malware can encode data such as encryption keys, biometric information, and files as radio signals that attackers intercept remotely using software-defined radio hardware and a simple antenna. The technique achieves data exfiltration speeds of 1000 bits per second, prompting the researchers to propose countermeasures against this type of air-gap breach.
