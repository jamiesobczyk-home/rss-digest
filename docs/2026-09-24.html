Your architecture diagram is not your resilience

Architecture diagrams document intended resilience but fail to reflect how systems actually operate after changes accumulate and drift from design occurs; hidden dependencies on infrastructure changes and, increasingly, on AI models that behave probabilistically compound this gap. Rather than treating resilience as a one-time project setup, organizations must continuously validate it through testing, infrastructure-as-code, and monitoring to prove that recovery paths still work as designed. Azure Infrastructure Resiliency Manager helps teams define resiliency goals, identify gaps between intended and actual designs, and systematically close them through automated assessment and remediation.
