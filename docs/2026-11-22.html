Harsher on Male? Evaluating LLMs on Gender-Asymmetric Moral Framing in Conflicts

Researchers created GAMA-Bench, a benchmark of 1,298 gender-paired conflict scenarios, to test whether 10 large language models apply consistent moral standards to the same misconduct when attributed to male versus female actors. Testing revealed a consistent male-disadvantaging bias: male actors received more punitive, escalatory, and blame-focused responses, while female actors received more empathetic and therapeutic framing for identical behavior. This disparity persisted across different model families, scenario types, model sizes, and reasoning approaches.

Notes on the discrete Fourier transform (DFT)

The Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) analyzes finite-duration discrete signals by treating N signal samples as one period of a periodic signal, enabling precise frequency reconstruction through the formula X[k] = Σ x[n]e^(-ik(2π/N)n). The DFT provides equally spaced samples of the continuous DTFT, making frequency analysis computationally practical for digital signal processing. The method connects to complex roots of unity, which form an orthogonal basis that underlies the DFT's mathematical structure.
