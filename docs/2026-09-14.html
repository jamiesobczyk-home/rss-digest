You’ve Definitely Taken a Photo Here at Disneyland, and Its Story Is Weirder Than You Think

In 1961, Walt Disney received marble statues of Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs from an anonymous Italian fan, but all figures were the same size. Designer John Hench solved the scale problem using forced perspective by placing Snow White at the top of a slope with the dwarfs below and a widening waterfall to enhance the illusion. The resulting Snow White Grotto became an iconic photo spot with a wishing well that donates proceeds to children's charities and has been replicated at Tokyo and Hong Kong Disneyland.

Pkgsrc Is Cool (2022)

pkgsrc is NetBSD's cross-platform package manager that supports building packages from source while keeping them isolated in separate directories like /usr/pkg or ~/pkg, preventing system pollution. OEMpocalypse: Unprivileged Android app to root on Samsung, Xiaomi, others

A security researcher has developed a generic exploitation strategy to escalate an unprivileged Android app to root access on devices from Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, OnePlus, and Realme by targeting page Use-After-Free vulnerabilities in OEM-specific kernel drivers. The approach combines a kernel-level page UAF exploit with OEM-specific sandbox escapes to bypass SELinux policies, prioritizing reliability across kernel versions and device variations over device-specific tweaks. The strategy has been instantiated across three major OEMs, covering Samsung flagship devices (S23–S26 and Z series), many Xiaomi mid-range to flagship devices, and recent flagship models from Oppo, OnePlus, and Realme.
