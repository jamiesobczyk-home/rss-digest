Three xAI founders want AI to predict the future

Three former xAI founders launched Epochal and created Time Portal, a 1T+ page web archive spanning 30+ years that lets AI agents relive the internet on specific dates to train predictive models, with system clocks and software matched to each target date to prevent detection of the replay. Testing on prediction market questions showed agents made nearly identical forecasts when replaying historical weeks, suggesting AI could achieve superhuman forecasting with enough past data. The startup, funded partially by $100M from co-founder Guodong Zhang, aims to give AI systems experiential learning through temporal replay rather than just reading archived content.

Taking over US Treasury subdomains for $3 each

Researchers exploited dangling DNS records to take over US Treasury subdomains by claiming AWS Elastic IP addresses ($3 each), finding 28 real vulnerabilities in just 48 hours before being banned from the service. The attack leverages a DNS misconfiguration where subdomains point to IP addresses that have been released back into AWS's available pool, allowing attackers to claim them and intercept traffic. Organizations are urged to review their DNS records immediately, as this practical attack remains viable in 2026 and could expose hundreds of vulnerable domains to determined attackers.
