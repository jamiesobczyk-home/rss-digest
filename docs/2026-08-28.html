Managed PostgreSQL vs. self-hosted PostgreSQL: Key benefits and trade-offs

Organizations choosing PostgreSQL must decide between self-hosting for full control or using managed services, which transfer infrastructure and platform responsibilities to the cloud provider. Self-hosting creates an "operational tax" of infrastructure management, security patching, high availability setup, and disaster recovery that consumes engineering resources better spent on applications. Managed PostgreSQL services are suitable when standardized operations, resilience, and faster deployment matter more than low-level infrastructure control, while self-hosting remains appropriate for teams needing specialized customization or possessing mature platform engineering capabilities.

Higher Education Does Not Need More AI Tools but Better AI Experiences

Higher education institutions no longer lack access to AI tools—the real challenge is implementing AI that fits faculty workflows and reduces friction rather than adding complexity. Faculty find AI most valuable for specific instructional tasks like generating quiz questions and drafting feedback, where it serves as a starting point that educators can review and refine rather than replacing their judgment. Successful AI adoption depends on embedding tools into familiar learning environments with clear policies, trusted platforms, and practical training that preserve educator agency.
