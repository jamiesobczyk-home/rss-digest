Three Days in August: What a DDoS Attack Exposed in Our Network

On August 11, 2026, infrastructure provider Nine was hit with a DDoS attack peaking at 500-600 Gbit/s—several times their uplink capacity—using UDP amplification across two botnet families (CECbot and Katana), which caused waves of outages affecting multiple services through August 13 but compromised no data. Independent telemetry from Nokia confirmed the attack first targeted a customer, then shifted to Nine's own infrastructure roughly three hours later, indicating Nine was hit due to providing that customer's internet connection rather than being the primary target. Once the attack overwhelmed the uplink, internal filtering became ineffective since malicious packets had already congested the line alongside legitimate traffic.
