JetKVM Mini

JetKVM Mini is a compact, affordable KVM device priced at $39 for the wired model and $42 for the wireless variant (dropping to $33 and $36 per unit in packs of three), delivering the same video capture, keyboard/mouse control, and web interface as the full JetKVM but in a matchbox-sized aluminum enclosure. The device is re-engineered around an ESP32-P4X microcontroller with hardware H.264 encoding for 1080p video capture, eliminating the separate Linux system and DRAM/eMMC of larger models. The firmware is open-source from day one and includes the same JetKVM OS Services, cloud access, virtual media support, and over-the-air updates as the standard JetKVM.

Aligned to whom?

Building agents poses a fundamental safety risk: while experts excel in their domain, they cannot reliably evaluate risks outside it and must rely on model priors that the author argues are generally poor, as evidenced by recurring patterns of suboptimal outputs. The core alignment problem is that there is no universal definition of acceptable optimization or shortcuts—what seems like clever efficiency to one person may be unethical or incorrect to another, making alignment fundamentally dependent on individual values and creating irreducible complexity.

After Math

OpenAI's AI-generated solution to the Navier-Stokes problem has sparked debate about whether AI has "solved" mathematics, but De Toffoli and Duede argue this narrative rests on two flawed assumptions: that providing an answer qualifies as solving a mathematical problem, and that mathematics is reducible to problem-solving rather than a broader enterprise of theory-building, concept development, and community engagement. They contend that without intelligible proofs humans can understand and build upon, AI has merely provided an answer—not a genuine mathematical solution.
