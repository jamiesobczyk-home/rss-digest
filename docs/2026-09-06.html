I Changed My License

Henri Bergius switched his default software license from MIT to EUPL-1.2, a strong copyleft license created by the European Union, because he believes permissive licenses have primarily benefited large corporations at the expense of developers and users. EUPL closes the "SaaS loophole" by requiring reciprocal licensing regardless of distribution method and includes official translations to 23 languages. He has already released several projects under EUPL, including reticulum-js, dacar, and signalk-energy-predictor.

You Don't Have a Right to Safe Drinking Water, US Court Rules

A federal appeals court ruled that the U.S. Constitution does not guarantee a right to safe drinking water, dismissing a lawsuit against Jackson, Mississippi for knowingly exposing residents to lead contamination; the court found no constitutional protection for truthful information during public health crises, despite similar protections existing in at least nine U.S. states and numerous countries. Jackson, an over 80 percent Black city with double the national poverty rate, concealed elevated lead levels discovered in 2015 for six months while residents drank toxic water, leading to lead poisoning diagnoses in several children who face permanent brain damage risks. The ruling coincides with the Trump administration's efforts to weaken Clean Water Act protections through new EPA rules.
