Next-Concept Modeling: Fast, Parallel Coarse-to-Fine Text Generation

Next-Concept Modeling (NCM) is a two-stage framework that encodes text into hierarchical discrete codes and generates them in parallel across levels using a transformer, achieving significant speedups over diffusion language models—21× faster on LM1B, 10× on OpenWebText, and 31× on XSum—while maintaining or improving generative quality. The method also outperforms autoregressive and diffusion baselines on reasoning tasks like Sudoku using fewer function evaluations.
