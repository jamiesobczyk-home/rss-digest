Faster than earcut: triangulating 3.7M map polygons in 489 ms

Globus published earstrip, a faster polygon triangulator for map rendering that is 1.4 to 2.1 times faster than earcut.js in the browser and 14% faster than earcut.hpp in C++. The library replaces a Delaunay triangulation algorithm with ear clipping, reducing triangulation time for 3.7 million map polygons from 2.93 seconds to 489 milliseconds. Ear clipping iteratively cuts off "ears" (triangular corners) from polygons until they are fully triangulated, proving particularly efficient for simple polygons common in map data.
