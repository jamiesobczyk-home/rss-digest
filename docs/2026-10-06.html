OpenAI Discontinuing Custom GPTs and Moving to Plugin Architecture

OpenAI is retiring Custom GPTs on December 11, 2026, replacing them with a plugin architecture that packages reusable skills, reference files, and connections to external services and MCP-based tools. Migration from existing GPTs is available but comes with limitations: conversations don't transfer, model selections don't carry over, and Custom Actions may require rebuilding, while migrated plugins start as private and don't automatically share with existing users. GPT builders have raised concerns that the new plugin architecture doesn't fully replicate the dedicated, predictable environment of Custom GPTs.

Gleam doesn't compile to Erlang source anymore

Gleam v1.19.0 now compiles to Erlang abstract forms instead of Erlang source code, enabling the compiler to bypass Erlang's front-end compilation and skip directly to runtime. This change significantly improves build times, ensures accurate line numbers in crash reports and stacktraces, and strengthens the compiler's codebase with modern standards. Benchmark tests show v1.19.0 compiles substantially faster than v1.17.0.

Old Hearts Become Biologically Younger When Transplanted Into Younger People

A preprint study on bioRxiv shows that transplanted hearts become biologically younger or older based on their recipient's age. Common mistakes you should avoid when using foldable phones

The most common mistake with foldable phones is closing the inner display on foreign objects like keys, coins, or dirt, which can scratch or puncture the screen. Avoid pressing heavily on the delicate inner screen, peeling off factory-applied protective film, or using unapproved screen protectors designed for standard phones.
