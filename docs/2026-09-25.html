Building a trusted foundation for confidential AI

Organizations increasingly need to use AI with sensitive data but can't expose that information to external services, creating a challenge that confidential AI addresses through hardware-isolated environments and cryptographic attestation. Hacker News · 2:31 AM

Amiga Screens: A Primer

An Amiga screen is a flexible display area with user-selectable resolution and color depth, unlike modern fixed-resolution monitors—a design that made sense on memory-constrained systems where different applications needed different display modes. The Amiga uses indexed color palettes with bitplanes to manage colors efficiently, where each bitplane adds another color index per pixel, allowing up to 32 colors on original hardware through five bitplanes or 256 colors on later AGA models. This hardware-enforced approach reflected the practical tradeoffs necessary when memory was expensive and systems needed to balance speed and performance across different use cases.
