AWS Architecture Blog · 6:13 AM

Consistency is the new latency: AI at the data layer

As AI agents scale into autonomous systems, data consistency becomes critical because stale reads from asynchronous replication lag cause agents to make incorrect decisions based on outdated information. The article outlines three architectural patterns—strong global consistency for high-stakes data, conditional writes for distributed concurrent updates, and local quorum consistency for high-velocity ingestion—to match database replication models to an agent's specific consistency requirements. The key insight is that AI reliability depends entirely on the data layer, making architects responsible for ensuring synchronized ground truth across all agent instances.

Nvidia Releases Nemotron 3.5 Lightning Open AI Model

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, a 30 billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates only 3 billion parameters per token, delivering four times faster output than similar models for use in long-running AI agent systems.
