Google Gemini 4 agents just beat Minecraft with no human intervention

Four Gemini 4 Argon AI agents autonomously defeated Minecraft's Ender Dragon by coordinating through in-game chat and shared memory, with each agent specializing in different tasks—gathering resources, crafting tools, and building shelters. The 27.23-hour achievement required the agents to write 2,783 programs, exchange 229 messages, and recover from 605 deaths while maintaining a coherent plan across thousands of dependent steps. This marks the first end-to-end autonomous completion of Minecraft by AI agents using only standard tools, demonstrating general agentic AI's capability in complex games that previously required custom software.

Tmux and Claude Code: Sessions, Send-Keys, and the Ceiling

tmux enables long-running AI coding agents to survive terminal disconnections and manage multiple agents in one window through scriptable session commands. Claude Code provides built-in automation for this via `claude --worktree --tmux`, which creates an isolated session for single-agent tasks; for more complex setups like multiple agents or remote scripting, manual tmux configuration is needed. Best practice is organizing sessions by project repository rather than individual tasks.

What do all the lights on your Netgear router mean, and which ones actually matter?

Netgear router lights indicate power status, internet connection, and Wi-Fi activity, with solid green or white typically meaning the device is operating normally and blinking lights showing data transmission or firmware updates. The most critical indicators are the power LED, internet LED, and Wi-Fi LED, while solid amber or blinking white often signal connecting states or potential issues like corrupted firmware. Additional lights for Wi-Fi bands and USB ports are useful for specific troubleshooting and device connections.

What does the orange dot on your Samsung Galaxy Watch mean?

The orange dot on Samsung Galaxy Watch displays unread notifications; a red dot indicates an urgent message instead. The indicator can be disabled by going to Settings > Notifications > Advanced Settings and toggling off "New notification indicator on watch face." Other symbols on the watch face convey connection status, lock status, and music/call activity.
