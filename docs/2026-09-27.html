Hacker News · 9:40 PM

What is the size of Yemen? (2024)

Yemen's commonly cited size of 555,000 sq km (and related estimates of 527,968 sq km used by the CIA, World Bank, and UN) traces back to century-old misinformation rather than accurate measurement. The 555k figure originated from a 1991 Yemeni statistical yearbook that inflated borders northward for propaganda purposes, while the 527k figure comes from Cold War-era estimates of North Yemen and South Yemen that were never corrected when sources were combined. When manually verified using Google Maps and Earth, Yemen's actual size is approximately 456,000 sq km—indicating these widely-cited figures have been perpetuated for decades without proper fact-checking.
