Impedance Matching

Impedance matching is a design principle that helps systems accept and transfer energy more efficiently by reducing resistance. Found everywhere from car transmissions and trumpet flares to anti-reflective lens coatings and recording booth foam, impedance matchers work by gradually transitioning properties to prevent wave reflection. The principle applies to sound, light, and other waves, and has potential applications ranging from musical instruments to climate engineering.

Hacker News · 4:26 AM

Speculative Decoding in vLLM on AMD GPUs

Speculative decoding in vLLM uses a draft-and-verify mechanism where a lightweight draft component proposes multiple candidate tokens and the target model verifies them in a single pass, improving output-token throughput compared to standard token-by-token autoregressive decoding. The technique preserves the original model's output behavior while reducing latency and serving constraints. The post evaluates five drafting approaches—native MTP, Gemma 4 MTP, EAGLE-3, DFlash, and DSpark—with performance measurements from AMD Instinct MI300X and MI355X GPUs.
