Press Release: Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2026

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine is jointly awarded to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for developing optogenetics, a method that uses light to control nerve cells. Hegemann and Nagel discovered channelrhodopsin, a protein in algae that opens ion channels when exposed to blue light; Deisseroth transformed this into a light-controlled switch for nerve cells in the brain. This breakthrough enables researchers to map neural circuits governing memories, feelings, and behaviors, with potential clinical applications for treating neurological disorders and restoring vision in visually impaired people.
