How Rakbank built centralized observability at scale with Amazon OpenSearch Service

Rakbank built a centralized observability platform on Amazon OpenSearch Service that ingests 150 GB of logs daily, using hot/warm/cold tiering with Index State Management to automatically age indices from hot EBS storage to S3-backed warm and cold tiers, achieving 55% cost savings versus an all-hot cluster while maintaining 7-year compliance retention. The cross-account architecture uses Fluent Bit to collect container logs from EKS and dual-write them to both OpenSearch for real-time search and S3 for durable backup, with private connectivity via AWS Transit Gateway and fine-grained access control via SAML federation. The design serves 25 concurrent users across 25 dashboards and delivers faster incident resolution, reduced mean time to resolution, and improved operational efficiency.

The spacecraft travels faster than its twin Voyager 2 due to trajectory choices made during early planetary flybys, reaching speeds of 3.5 astronomical units per year compared to Voyager 2's 3.1.
