The treasury bond mess: is this the demise of the US as a safe haven?

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's attempt to stabilize the bond market by increasing government purchases of treasury bonds failed to push yields down, as they quickly bounced back to previous highs. Rising treasury yields since 2022 have sharply increased the cost of servicing the $40 trillion federal debt—now consuming 13.5% of federal spending—while damaging the broader economy and Trump's popularity.
