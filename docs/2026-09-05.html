Sep 4, 2026ScienceFormalizing Fermat's Last Theorem

Anthropic researchers used Claude to produce the first complete computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 11 days, with the AI working largely autonomously to write the proof in the Lean programming language while generating 13 million lines of code and proving 29,500 intermediate theorems. The achievement demonstrates that AI can successfully formalize highly complex mathematical proofs, which could significantly reduce the time and effort required to verify new mathematical results. This autoformalization capability represents a major step toward making all mathematics readily verifiable by computers.
