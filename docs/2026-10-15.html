Cryptographic systems rarely fail due to weak mathematics but rather from delays in deploying security patches across complex supply chains—a gap called the "last mile of trust." The Double Headed Stack Allocator

A stack allocator (bump allocator) uses a simple integer counter for memory allocation—allocation is just adding to the counter, and deallocation resets it to zero, making both operations negligible for performance. The author implemented this at Surreal Software around 2010 to solve severe bottlenecks in animation blending for a game, achieving dramatic performance gains where allocation time became unmeasurable. Stack allocators work efficiently in multi-threaded environments with atomic operations and support partial deallocations for recursive algorithms.
