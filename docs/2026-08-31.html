Study: Blue light impairs the eye's ability to distinguish fine detail most

A University of Georgia study found that blue light from smartphones, computers, and televisions impairs the eye's ability to distinguish fine detail more than other wavelengths due to increased light scatter and optical aberrations. People with lighter-colored eyes experience greater light scatter than those with darker brown eyes because lighter irises contain less melanin to absorb excess light. The researchers recommend using blue light filtering features or glasses, particularly for people who spend long hours on digital devices, since prolonged exposure is linked to oxidative stress and potential macular degeneration over time.
