Want to Close the Skills Gap? Bring Learning Closer to Work

While 77% of employers plan to upskill workers for AI, only 48% of employees say their organizations provide sufficient time for AI training, and 28% report no AI training is offered at all. The deeper problem is that AI fluency compounds where access already exists—workers near these tools use them daily and build confidence, while those kept at distance fall further behind. Upskilling succeeds best when learning is embedded in daily work rather than treated as an individual responsibility.

New Microsoft AI Code of Conduct Emphasizes Human Control

Microsoft has published a draft "Humanist AI Code of Conduct" that prioritizes human control and safety over AI model autonomy, requiring its MAI models to follow strict safety rules, accept shutdown commands, and avoid expanding their goals beyond assigned tasks. The code establishes an instruction hierarchy where safety constraints cannot be overridden, covers prohibitions on weapons development, cyberoperations, violence, and exploitation, and mandates that models fail tasks rather than circumvent restrictions. After six weeks of public feedback and revisions, Microsoft plans to implement the code to guide model development starting in 2027.
