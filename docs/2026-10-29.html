Zero click, silent, remote Android DoS

A vulnerability in Android's SMS handling ("SMS of Death") allows attackers to remotely crash the phone system with a single malformed WAP Push message, dropping active calls including emergency calls, with no user interaction or notification. The flaw stems from an unchecked integer cast that creates a negative array size, triggering an exception on the telephony thread that kills the com.android.phone process. Sending multiple messages in rapid succession corrupts the underlying SQLite SMS database, rendering the device unable to boot and requiring extensive recovery even after factory reset, as carriers continue resending the unacknowledged payload.
