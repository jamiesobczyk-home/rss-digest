NEWS: Flight Prices Could Stay High, Even if Fuel Prices Drop

Flight prices may remain elevated even if fuel costs decline because airlines book flights and sell tickets months in advance; when fuel prices spike after seats are already sold, airlines cannot retroactively raise fares and must absorb the higher costs, making them reluctant to lower prices if fuel becomes cheaper.
