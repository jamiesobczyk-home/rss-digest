How Dynata scaled AI across the development lifecycle with AI-DLC and Kiro IDE

Dynata reduced feature development time from 3–4 sprints to approximately one sprint by implementing the AI-Driven Development Lifecycle (AI-DLC) methodology using Kiro IDE, which brings AI into every phase of software delivery from requirements through implementation. The approach uses a structured three-day workshop where cross-functional teams collaborate to resolve requirements gaps and design decisions during planning—before code is written—rather than discovering them during implementation. The methodology achieved ~70% reduction in development time, fewer post-production bugs, and unified team alignment in a single day instead of days or weeks of iterative meetings.

Slashdot · 2:00 AM

World's Timekeepers Vote to Eliminate the Leap Second

Timekeepers have voted to eliminate leap seconds, which have been added occasionally to keep Coordinated Universal Time aligned with Earth's rotation, ending a 30-year debate. The change replaces the leap second with a theoretical "leap hour" that won't be necessary for hundreds or thousands of years, avoiding computer system failures like the Reddit crash caused by a leap second in 2012. The move was prompted by Earth's spin acceleration making a negative leap second possible for the first time, raising fears similar to Y2K concerns about technology compatibility.

Hacker News · 12:11 AM

The Folders Programming Language

Folders is a non-textual esoteric programming language where code is expressed purely through directory structure and containment, with folder names ignored except for alphabetical ordering. Created to explore "dematerialized code," it leverages Windows' handling of empty folders (which consume no disk space) to theoretically offer infinite storage capacity. Commands and expressions are encoded through the number and arrangement of nested subfolders, with programs visualizable as trees, contour maps, or other structural representations.
