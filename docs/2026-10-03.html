1 In 8 Cancer Cases Worldwide Are Due to Infections

An estimated 2.3 million cancer cases globally in 2024—about 12% of all cancers—are attributable to infections, primarily H. pylori, HPV, HBV, EBV, and HCV, according to research published in The Lancet Oncology. Over three-quarters of these infection-related cancers occur in low- and middle-income countries, with Eastern Asia accounting for 42% of the global total. The finding highlights cancer as a largely preventable disease through aggressive infection control, vaccination, and treatment strategies.

Hacker News · 10:15 PM

Cloudflare OHTTP gateway

Cloudflare is launching an OHTTP Gateway, a privacy infrastructure service that allows app backends to receive HTTP requests without seeing user IP addresses, complementing their existing OHTTP Relay product. The gateway works alongside a relay through separated "hops"—where the relay hides client identifiers and the gateway decrypts requests—ensuring no single entity sees both user identity and request contents. Available as a paid add-on, it addresses developer demand for accessible privacy infrastructure and serves use cases where app servers already run on Cloudflare.
