The Making of Oxygene

In late 1976, 28-year-old French musician Jean-Michel Jarre released *Oxygène*, a groundbreaking six-track instrumental electronic album that topped charts in seven countries and sold nearly 20 million copies worldwide. The album's creation was influenced by Jarre's studies with pioneering composers at Paris's GRM studio and Stockhausen in Cologne, combined with his goal to bridge experimental music with pop melody using a home-built setup centered on the VCS3 synthesizer. Entirely synthesizer-based with no acoustic instruments, *Oxygène* revolutionized electronic music and redefined what synthesizers could achieve.
