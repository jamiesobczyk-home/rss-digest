Physicists Trap a Single Antimatter Particle For a Month

Northwestern University physicists successfully trapped a single positron for a month by slowing it through tungsten and holding it in a vacuum using electromagnetic and magnetic fields. The team plans to measure the positron's magnetic moment and compare it to the electron's measurements to test a prediction of the Standard Model of particle physics. If the measurements differ, it could help explain why the universe is composed of matter rather than antimatter.
